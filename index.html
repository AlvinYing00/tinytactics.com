FIELD GUIDE

Know your team.

  1. Build a team. Start with 10 gold, level 3 and three 1-cost champions. Buy from the shop, then drag units onto your half. Your level sets your deployment limit.
  2. Prepare, then fight. Eight players compete. Each round has 30s preparation and a 3s face-off. After every board finishes, a 1.5s pause leads to the next round. Scout other players anytime.
  3. Manage gold. Earn 5 base gold per round, up to 5 interest, plus win and streak bonuses. Rerolls cost 2 gold. Lock to keep offers next round. During combat, only bench champions can be sold or combined.
  4. Upgrade. Three identical copies combine. 2★ has 175% HP / 150% Attack; 3★ has 300% HP / 225% Attack, based on 1★ stats. Level up beside the board to deploy more.
  5. Survive. Losses cost stage damage plus enemy survivors, subject to the stage cap. Every seventh round brings monsters: win to heal, lose to take damage. Last player standing wins.
  6. Experiment in Sandbox. Build both teams freely, set stars, and choose up to five Combat, Trait or Class Augments per side. No gold or player HP. Reset restores formations and keeps Augments.

Distinct deployed champions count once. Bench units and summons do not count; Augments can add counts. The highest reached tier applies, except Air needs an exact count. Tap an element for its breakpoints.

All classes activate at 2 / 4 / 6. Cosmic borrows Tanker, Duelist, Assassin or Ranger effects from starting rows 1–4, without adding a class count. Stun and Freeze stop movement and attacks; Fear stops attacks only.

Choose 1 of 3 at 1-4, 2-4, 3-4, 4-4 and 5-4 during normal preparation. Reroll each card once. Timeout picks randomly; chosen Augments never return.

Economy: Gold, XP, gifts and shop benefits.

Combat: Team, class or formation bonuses.

Trait: Element counts and special powers.

Class: Extra class counts; Chests also gift champions.

Gifts ignore the shop pool. Overflow gifts sell at fight start unless you make room. Cosmic, Dragon and Universe Orbs gift champions; ordinary elemental Orbs add counts.
