FIELD GUIDE

Recruit. Position. Battle.

  1. Build your team

    Both players begin at level 3 with 100 HP and 10 gold. Buy shop cards and drag champions from the nine-slot bench into your lower four rows. Your level is your deployment limit. Drop on a teammate to swap. Starting battle automatically fills open board slots from the bench, prioritizing distinct champions sharing your deployed traits. Existing placements stay in place.

  2. Manage your gold

    Refresh five shop cards for 2 gold, or wait for a free new shop next round. Each round gives 5 base gold, plus 1 per 10 gold held before rewards (maximum 5). Winners get 1 extra. Consecutive losses pay 1 extra at three, 2 at four, and 3 at five or more. A win or draw resets the loss streak. Draws deal no player damage.

  3. Grow your company

    Both players earn 2 XP after every round. The next levels require 10 / 20 / 30 / 40 / 55 / 65 / 80 XP. Each XP earned reduces the remaining gold price by 1: 2/10 XP means 8 gold to level up. Reaching the requirement levels you automatically and carries extra XP forward. Level 10 is the maximum. Higher levels improve future shop odds.

  4. Combine your champions

    Three identical 1-star copies automatically become one 2-star champion. Three identical 2-star copies become one 3-star champion, the maximum. Copies can be on the board or bench; different costs or identities do not combine. An upgraded champion keeps a deployed copy's tile when possible. Two stars add 25% of the role's 1-cost base HP and attack damage; three stars add 50%, without stacking the previous bonus. Selling refunds all copies invested: 1 / 3 / 9 times the card cost.

  5. Win the match

    Start combat after preparing. Champions automatically move and fight. All owned champions return at full health next round. Rounds run 1-1 through 1-7, then 2-1 and onward. The losing player takes 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 HP damage in stages 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5+. Reduce Ember to 0 HP to win the match. A 60-second battle is a draw.

  6. Fire & Water

    Distinct deployed champions count once. Fire 2/4/6/8 burns 5/10/15/20% maximum HP each second for 3 seconds; attacks refresh it. Fire 10 continuously launches a meteor every 0.5 seconds. Each takes 3 seconds to fall, deals 25% maximum HP damage and hits at most two enemies. Water 3/6/9 heals its attacker for 5/15/25% maximum HP per attack. Water 10 adds a seven-second tsunami carrying enemies off the right edge. Maximum traits retain their strongest normal tier.

  7. Mountain & Electric

    Mountain 1/3/5/7 grants Mountain champions a shield worth 10/25/50/100% of their maximum HP at round start. Mountain 9 also gives five seconds of golden immunity to burns, tsunami sweeps and stuns. A resisted wave cannot catch the same champion later. Electric 3/6/9 adds 5/10/20% of star-adjusted attack damage to Electric champions. Electric 10 strikes every enemy once at five seconds for 50% maximum HP; enemies already below 50% HP are executed through shields.

  8. Air

    Air 1/4 gives each Air attack a 25/50% chance to collect one gold immediately. Air 10 creates a wind wall across the middle for five seconds: enemies cannot cross it or target Air champions with basic attacks. Air can still attack. Air champions gain 50% of their current star-adjusted HP and attack for the whole battle, resetting at round end. Class bonuses are coming later.

Keyboard: Tab to a champion, Enter to select, then Enter on its destination to move or swap. Escape clears selection. Space starts or pauses when a board tile is focused. Match progress lasts until you reload or start a new match.
