FIELD GUIDE

Recruit. Position. Battle.

  1. Build your team

    All eight players begin at level 3 with 100 HP, 10 gold and three random 1-cost champions. Each preparation lasts 30 seconds. The enemy board appears after preparation, followed by a three-second face-off before combat. Buy shop cards and drag champions from the nine-slot bench into your lower four rows. Your level is your deployment limit. Drop on a teammate to swap, or drag to the sell area beside the bench heading for gold. Clicking only inspects champions. Starting battle automatically fills open board slots from the bench, prioritizing distinct champions sharing your deployed traits. Existing placements stay in place.

  2. Manage your gold

    Open your shop at any time while alive to recruit or reroll for 2 gold. Drag to the sell area, or click it to manage your champions even while scouting. During face-off, combat and the waiting phase, only bench champions can be sold. Deployed champions can be sold during preparation. Lock preserves the remaining offers through the next round, then automatically unlocks. You can lock again during preparation. Manual rerolls still work while locked. Each round gives 5 base gold, plus 1 per 10 gold held before rewards (maximum 5). Winners get 1 extra. Win and loss streaks pay +1 at 2–4, +2 at 5, and +3 at 6 or more. A draw resets both streaks.

  3. Grow your company

    All players earn 2 XP after every round. The next levels require 6 / 10 / 20 / 36 / 60 / 68 / 68 XP. Each XP earned reduces the remaining gold price by 1: 2/6 XP means 4 gold to level up. Reaching the requirement levels you automatically and carries extra XP forward. Level 10 is the default maximum. Higher and higher unlocks level 11 for 76 XP; Quick Formation caps you at level 7. Higher levels improve future shop odds.

  4. Choose Augments

    Choose one of three Augments during the normal 30-second preparation. Economy choices appear at 1-4, 2-4 and 5-4; Combat choices appear at 2-4, 3-4 and 5-4. Combat offers match your currently deployed classes or formation. Team-wide bonuses work with any deployed champion. Reroll each card once; offers never repeat in that selection. Previously chosen Augments do not return. An unanswered selection is picked randomly when preparation ends. Gift copies ignore shop availability. Overflow gifts wait on the board: move them to an open team or bench slot to keep them, or they sell at fight start. Trait and Class counts appear at 2-4, 3-4 and 4-4. Special Trait Augments appear at 4-4 and 5-4 only when their related breakpoint is active. Inspect selected Augments beside your board or while scouting another player.

  5. Shared champion pool

    All eight players draw from the same pool. Each champion has 30 / 25 / 18 / 10 / 9 copies at costs 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5. Champions with fewer available copies appear less often within their cost tier. Shop offers reserve copies, including locked shops. Rerolling returns unbought cards; selling returns the copies originally drawn from the pool. Augment-generated copies do not use or add to the pool. Eliminated players return their cards. Owned 3-star champions stop appearing in your shop. An exhausted cost tier gives an empty slot. An Augment can unlock level 11.

  6. Combine your champions

    Three identical 1-star copies automatically become one 2-star champion. Three identical 2-star copies become one 3-star champion, the maximum. During combat, only bench copies can combine. Board upgrades wait for preparation. Copies can be on the board or bench during preparation; different costs or identities do not combine. An upgraded champion keeps a deployed copy's tile when possible. At every cost, 2★ has 175% HP / 150% attack and 3★ has 300% HP / 225% attack. Both scale directly from that champion’s 1★ stats, never the previous star tier. Selling refunds all copies invested: 1 / 3 / 9 times the card cost.

  7. Win the match

    Four independent fights run at once with eight players alive. Wait for every board to finish, then a 1.5-second transition starts the next 30-second preparation. Your shop opens automatically. All owned champions return at full health. Losing costs 2 / 2 / 5 / 8 / 10 / 12 / 17 base HP at stages 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 / 7+, plus 1 per enemy survivor. Total PvP damage is capped at 3 / 5 / 7 / 9 / 12 / 15 / 18 HP respectively. Combat runs at 1× for 30 real seconds, 2× for the next 15, then 3× for the last 15. Sandbox keeps manual speed controls. A double wipe or 60-second real-time timeout is a draw: both take base damage. The last company standing wins. Opponents rotate within a stage, restarting after eliminations; an odd survivor faces a frozen copy of another board.

  8. Scouting & monster rounds

    Click a company in the lobby to watch its formation, bench, or live combat. Scouted benches are read-only. All battles keep running while scouting. Bots compare formations using only scoutable boards. Every seventh round is a monster encounter: 3 wolves, 2 tigers, a bear, a dragon, then a random 3-star legendary. Wins restore 2 / 4 / 8 / 12 / 20 HP; losses cost 1 / 2 / 4 / 6 / 10 HP across stages 1–5+.

  9. Trait counts & shop

    Active traits appear as circular icons beside the board; the number is your trait count. Click an icon for its breakpoints. Active champions glow; MAX traits decorate each team’s own half without affecting combat, except Nature which has no board artwork. Distinct deployed champions count once; bench champions do not count. The shop has 60 champions, including Asterion. Dragonoid and Elyndra are Augment-only (all 62 are in Sandbox). The non-Cosmic roster has 6 Mountain, 7 Fire, 7 Water, 6 Electric, 8 Air, 4 Nature, 7 Light, 7 Dark and 7 Ice. Cost tiers 1–5 contain 7 / 15 / 12 / 10 / 16 champions. Fire 7 and Mountain 6 are naturally reachable. Electric 7, Air 10, Water 9/10, Light 8, Dark 8 and Ice 10 need additional trait counts. Non-Cosmic legendaries count toward their class bonuses. Cosmic gains Tanker / Duelist / Assassin / Ranger effects from its starting row, without adding class count. Cosmic MAX terrain takes priority. Owned 3-star champions are excluded from your shop. Deployment is normally capped at 10; Higher and higher unlocks 11.

  10. Nature

    At 2/4 Nature, from two seconds onward, Nature champions gain 1/2% of current max HP and attack every second. Each gain compounds and heals the HP gained. Nature 4 also summons one doppelganger per Nature champion with 50% of its owner’s starting HP and attack. Copies fight normally, stay at half-strength, and do not add trait counts or deployment slots. Surviving copies count as enemy survivors. All growth and copies reset next round.

  11. Fire & Water

    Fire 1/3/5 applies 5/7/10% maximum-HP Burn per second for three seconds. New hits refresh it without stacking. Fire 7 retains 10% Burn and creates one Meteor per enemy: staggered launches, three-second flights, 25% maximum-HP damage, and up to two enemies per impact. Water 3/6/9 heals its attacker for 5/15/25% maximum HP per basic attack. Water 10 adds a seven-second Tsunami: enemies are carried right and cannot move or attack while swept. Caught enemies are swept off the board and marked OUT without dying, so removal does not trigger Electric inheritance.

  12. Mountain

    Mountain 1/2/4/6 starts combat with shields worth 10/25/50/100% maximum HP. At 2/4/6, reflect 10/20/30% of basic-attack damage that actually reaches current HP. Shield damage, overkill and trait damage never reflect. Reflection cannot reflect again or trigger on-hit effects. Mountain 6 adds five seconds of Golden Shield against Burn, stun, Freeze, Fear, Chill and Tsunami displacement; normal attack damage still applies.

  13. Electric

    Electric 3/5 grants every surviving Electric ally 5/10% of each fallen Electric champion’s original round-start maximum HP and base attack, healing the HP gained. Inherited bonuses never transfer again. Electric 7 grants 25% HP and 15% attack inheritance and triggers Thunder Judgment once at three seconds: 25% maximum-HP damage to every enemy, or execution through shields if already below 25% HP.

  14. Air — exact counts only

    Only exactly 1/4/8/10 Air activates the trait; every other count disables all Air bonuses. Coin chances per basic attack are 5/7.5/10/15%. At 4/8/10, dodge chances are 5/10/15%, and every 10th/8th/5th attack deals 2×/2.5×/3× basic damage respectively. Each champion tracks its own counter, reset every combat. Dodges prevent basic damage and target on-hit effects; completed attacks still count, roll coins and trigger attacker self-healing.

  15. Wind Pierce & Wind Wall

    Air 10 basic attacks also hit every enemy in the line behind the target, continuing to the board edge in the attack direction—including diagonals. Empty tiles and allies do not block the line. Each enemy takes 50% of the original attack’s actual HP and shield damage, with no reduction for distance or earlier pierced targets. Critical damage counts; overkill does not. A dodged attack does not pierce. Pierce is trait damage: it cannot be dodged, reflected or trigger another attack. Air 10 also places a wall between teams for five seconds. Neither team can cross it. Enemy basic attacks cannot target Air across it; the protected Air team can attack through it. No HP or attack boost is granted. Trait damage bypasses the wall. Temporary combat effects reset at round end.

  16. Class traits: 2 / 4 / 6

    Tankers reduce champion damage by 10/20/30%; trait damage is unaffected. Duelists gain 3/4/5% attack speed per completed attack, capped at 15/30/50%. Rangers deal 10/20/35% more basic damage from at least three tiles away; Ranger 6 adds one range. Only actual class traits count. Supports heal the 1/2/3 lowest-health allies for 5/7.5/10% max HP every five seconds; Support 6 adds a 5% shield for three seconds. Assassins attempt one opening jump, prioritizing Rangers then Supports. Wind Wall blocks crossing and the jump never retries. After a successful jump, enemy Rangers and Supports focus reachable Tankers and Duelists first. At 2/4/6, Assassins deal 15/30/50% more damage to Rangers and Supports for five seconds; at 4/6 a successful jump grants 10/20% dodge for five seconds. Assassin 6 also sends 25% of basic damage directly through Ranger/Support shields.

Keyboard: Tab to a champion and Enter to inspect. Use the selection panel’s Sell button as an alternative to dragging. Escape clears selection. Space starts or pauses Sandbox battles when a board tile is focused. Fight mode advances automatically. Match progress lasts until you reload or start a new match.
